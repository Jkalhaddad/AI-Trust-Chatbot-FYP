USER: "What are the limtations or ways it can still fail?"
BOT: Two-factor authentication (2FA) improves security but isn’t foolproof. Limitations include:

1. **Phishing Attacks:** Hackers can trick you into revealing both your password and 2FA code.
2. **SIM Swapping:** Attackers hijack your phone number to receive 2FA codes sent via SMS.
3. **Lost Device:** If your second-factor device (like your phone) is lost or inaccessible, you might be locked out.
4. **Malware:** Some malware can capture your 2FA codes or bypass authentication.
5. **User Mistakes:** Users might disable 2FA or use weak second factors.

Despite these, 2FA significantly reduces the risk compared to password-only security.
