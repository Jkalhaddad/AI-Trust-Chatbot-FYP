USER: Explain your reasoning 
BOT: Certainly! HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) ensures that the data transferred between your browser and the website is encrypted, protecting it from being intercepted or tampered with by third parties. This means when you see HTTPS, your connection is secure.

However, HTTPS does not verify the authenticity or trustworthiness of the website itself.
